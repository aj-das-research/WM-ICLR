## Paper and qualitative evidence

The original main/appendix comparisons can now be finalized from the complete source ledger. Positive cases can explain what changed in the observed execution—such as lower task-relevant endpoint error or reaching the goal sooner—while preserving the exact saved frames, matched goals, native-action timing and failure examples. They cannot establish that context factorization caused the improvement; that needs the ongoing matched-control study. The highlighted PushT win has worse local prediction error in all five measured pairs; the highlighted Reacher win has lower error in three of five. This does not establish a uniform causal mechanism or CEM candidate-ranking improvement. See `reports/qualitative_prediction_diagnostics.md`.
