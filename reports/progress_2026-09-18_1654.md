## Reusable checkpoint inventory

Six packages belong to the proposed paired-factorized method: PushT/Reacher × seeds 0, 1 and 2. The other 24 packages are trained controls. Each package is approximately 95 MiB and includes inference weights, config, model card, pinned source wheel, provenance, logged metrics and verification. They are LeWM fine-tunes and conditioning modules, not 30 different foundation architectures.

Paths: `artifacts/releases/{environment}_{mode}_s{seed}/`; modes are `factorized`, `factorized_unpaired`, `single`, `framewise`, `plain`. Each environment has seeds 0–2. The loader API is `shiftwm.checkpoint.load_package`.

All 30 packages passed their recorded portable CPU weights-only loading and real-support rollout checks; the parallel audit verified all 388 package-file hashes against manifests. This verifies loading/compatibility, not benchmark superiority. Checkpoint selection uses validation-best loss after all 30 epochs, rather than automatically releasing epoch 30. Best/final states and demo copies do not count as additional trained models. Inference packages exclude optimizer state.

## What completed results establish

On held-out composition, paired factorization reduces forecast MSE@5 versus framewise calibration by 3.68% on PushT and 26.78% on Reacher. On extrapolation it reduces PushT error by 4.20% but raises Reacher error by 4.15%. These are three-training-seed prediction comparisons, not a planning guarantee.

The completed seed-0 paired-factorized Reacher main test has 222/576 successes (38.54%) versus frozen 190/576 (32.99%). The full aligned-control and three-seed planning comparison remains incomplete. The unaligned diagnostic and extrapolation outcomes remain in the raw inventory; one favorable comparison does not establish a method advantage.

Development with the official budget: PushT frozen/shared/factorized/framewise each 1/31 eligible successes. Reacher: 3/30, 6/30, 6/30 and 11/30, respectively. Goal-only hybrid: PushT 0/31; Reacher 12/30. These are exploratory seed-0 development results, separate from main test.

Scope so far: one pretrained backbone family (LeWM), two simulated environments, photometric and dynamics shifts. Cross-backbone, real-robot, clinical and general-agent benefits have not been established.
