## Actual learned quantities and honest maps

Store per-seed predictions, patch standardized squared errors, row-stochastic T, gate g, correction Δ, effective M=(1−g)I+gT and normalized last-observation anchor. Reconstruct every forecast as MZ0+Δ and validate against the normal prediction API. For a diagnostic target row use fixed zero-based index5 (row2,column2), consistent across cases; retain full16×16 arrays so the figure does not depend on one chosen patch. Also retain all16-patch own-location means, gate means and correction RMS.

Average each seed's squared error and each seed's effective M. Do not take error of an averaged prediction or reconstruct M from averaged g/T. M's diagonal increase over T is algebraic by construction, not evidence of performance benefit. These are learned **source-mixing weights**, not semantic attention, saliency, optical flow or demonstrated physical correspondence. Error maps live on globally contextualized DINO spatial features and are not pixel-level object-error labels.

Use the same4×4 geometry, full source photographs, crop policy and shared zero-to-maximum error scale for all compared methods/cases within a study/task. Use0–1 for T/g/M. Preserve signed corrections in source arrays; any RMS magnitude map must be labeled as such and share one scale. Do not compare raw color magnitudes across tasks with different fitted normalization. No fabricated uncertainty bands or RGB forecasts.

## Derived figure API

`derived.json` schema `qualitative_closest_derived_v1` contains `studies[]`, each with scope/method identities, actual offsets/display offsets, common scales and `cases[]`. Each case has the fixed selection record, intact local image references/hashes, and `methods[mode]` with three `per_seed` records plus a `mean`. Numeric records contain `mse_curve` and `by_offset` maps: `patch_mse4x4`, and for mixing models `T16x16`, `M16x16`, `gate4x4`, `correction_rms4x4`. Shapes are4×4 for patch/gate/RMS,16×16 forT/M. DROID offsets5/10 and IWS14/29/44/59 are retained; full arrays remain local. There are no feature vectors, predicted RGB, commands or downloadable raw-image assets in the public numeric pack. The parent may embed a few reduced-resolution attributed IWS research excerpts in an annotated paper figure with the unspecified dataset-license status disclosed.

Numerical replay tolerances are fixed before execution: per-window standardized-MSE curve versus finalized ledger rtol2e−5/atol2e−6; derived patch/global MSE rtol2e−6/atol2e−7; MZ0+Δ reconstruction in float64 versus actual FP32 prediction rtol5e−6/atol2e−6. The IWS replay uses batch1 versus the source evaluator batch64 and records actual differences. Ordinary and return-details predictions must be bitwise equal for identical CPU8/thread/batch1 inputs. No fallback or tolerance escalation. Correction-magnitude means average the three independently calculated seed RMS values; they are not pooled RMS.
