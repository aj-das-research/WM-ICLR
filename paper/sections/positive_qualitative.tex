\subsection{Additional goal-reaching examples}
Figure~\ref{fig:qual-positive-two} completes the positive cases from
Figure~\ref{fig:technical-contract}: together they show all four
policy-eligible development tasks on which the historical ShiftWM (ours)
context model succeeds and Framewise fails. Shared context fails on each.
The full eligible comparison has one versus one discordant success on PushT
and three versus eight on Reacher; these selected cases explain outcomes,
not average superiority. Counterexamples follow, and the gallery retains
every task.

Identical per-case crops and display-only goal outlines expose the remaining
joint mismatch. Call-20 frames align observation times, while endpoints retain
the actual stopping times. The endpoint joint-error plots show why visual
closeness or a single L2 threshold does not determine success: both unwrapped
joint errors must be below 0.05 radians. These observations support a
behavioral explanation without attributing success to a particular context
or candidate ranking. Appendix~\ref{sec:rollout-revision} separately examines
objective and context interventions.
