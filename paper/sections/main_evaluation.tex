\section{Evaluation design}
\label{sec:editorial-evaluation}
\paragraph{Matched spatial forecasting.}
The DROID collection contains 1,126 selected recordings, divided by recording
session into 851 training, 143 validation and 132 original test episodes.
The spatial development study uses only the original training/validation
partition. At ten query steps, 141 validation episodes from 59 sessions yield
1,631 eligible windows. The native target is a common, training-standardized
$4\times4$ grid; pooled $2\times2$ coordinates are a supplementary metric.
The autoregressive control, anchoring variants and ablations share the frozen
features, history and command access except for each declared removed path.
Persistence copies the last observation.

The initial spatial campaign trains 15 models for 30 epochs. A registered
follow-up adds six models to complete the mixing-by-bounding comparison after
the initial controls were revealed. Every arm has three seeds. Checkpoint
selection uses window-weighted validation loss over all ten query steps;
reported metrics average windows within episodes, then weight episodes and
seeds equally. Paired intervals resample recording sessions and training
seeds with 10,000 draws. These are exploratory development comparisons without
multiplicity correction. Full recipes, checkpoints, all horizons and all
36 effects are in Appendix~\ref{app:spatial-development}.

\paragraph{Original simulation and real-video protocols.}
Visual PushT and Reacher vary appearance and physical response, withholding
one combination while exposing both factors separately in training.
Extrapolation introduces new factor values. The original models train for
30 epochs with three seeds, and planning shares a 50-native-step budget
including support acquisition. Framewise calibration, shared context and
unpaired contexts are matched controls on the same backbone; Frozen LeWM is
the released reference. These complete primary results and the composition
grid remain in Appendix~\ref{app:original-context-study}.

The earlier real-video context study uses the original session-separated
DROID test partition. A subsequent confirmation freezes models and
training-fitted calibration before decoding 65 additional episodes from
52 new sessions. These fixed-test results are reported separately from
spatial development (Appendices~\ref{app:real-video} and
\ref{app:fresh-real-video}). Models, representations and forecast lengths
are matched within each study; scores from different studies are not pooled.
