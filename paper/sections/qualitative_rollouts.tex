\section{Matched qualitative executions}
\label{sec:qualitative-rollouts}
Figures~\ref{fig:qual-pusht-contrasts}--\ref{fig:qual-reacher-controls}
show actual simulator observations from the completed seed-zero development
evaluations at $(v_1,p_1)$. We select the lexicographically first task in each
outcome stratum relative to Framewise calibration: ShiftWM-only success,
Framewise-only success, and joint failure. The fourth example is joint success
for Reacher; PushT has no eligible joint success, so we explicitly show a
support-only success instead. Selection is by this stated rule, not visual
quality; these outcome-stratified examples do not estimate success frequency.
The accompanying local gallery contains all 64 tasks and adds Shared context.

Both methods receive pixel-identical initial observations and available goals,
with the same planner seed and budget: 300 candidates, 30 CEM iterations,
30 elites, horizon five action blocks, and at most 50 native calls including
support. The first saved frame follows support acquisition; subsequent frames
follow executed blocks of up to five actions. Labels reconstruct exact native
times, including early stopping. Repeated terminal observations do not imply
additional interactions. The world model predicts latents; none of the displayed
images is a decoded prediction, interpolated state, or generated illustration.
