Ours-2--4 are component controls, not independently proposed algorithms;
Ours-1 denotes the earlier, distinct two-context model. Stable identifiers
below preserve checkpoint and result-file correspondence. Their numbering
never implies a performance ranking. All spatial arms have three-seed results.
