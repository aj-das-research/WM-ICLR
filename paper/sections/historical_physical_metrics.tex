\subsection{Physical outcomes and interaction cost in the historical studies}
\label{app:historical-physical-metrics}
We supplement saved historical-model success outcomes with final physical error
and actual interaction cost. This is a secondary analysis of completed traces,
not another simulator run or a result for the current spatial architecture.
The analysis preserves all nine core test conditions, three extrapolation
conditions, and separate extension backbones. All 24,944 recorded
task--training-seed--condition outcomes remain in the source ledger.

\paragraph{Success, distance and effort answer different questions.}
In the full PushT test grid, historical ShiftWM achieves 6.77\% success versus
5.96\% for Framewise, while its mean final joint pusher/block positional error is
higher (316.35 versus 262.64 native pixels). Reacher success is tied at 38.95\%,
but wrapped joint error is 0.320 versus 0.285 radians. Thus, a positive success
point difference does not imply better final geometry. These findings motivate
reporting both quantities in the new current-model planning study.

\paragraph{Denominators and uncertainty.}
Every attempted native command includes the ten paid support commands. Failed
trials remain in the success-by-budget denominator. Mean attempted calls alone
can reward early failure, so the source also reports success-only calls and a
budget-penalized score that assigns the full budget to failures. Core intervals
resample paired initial-state clusters conditional on the three fixed training
seeds; extension intervals resample both task and training seeds. New continuous
physical-error contrasts are unadjusted post-hoc analyses. Neither units nor
backbones are pooled. The complete distribution supplement and per-condition
CSV files accompany the reproducible artifacts.
