\paragraph{Reading the numerical scorecards.}
Each result table names the method and backbone, uses a fixed population within
each comparison group, and identifies the metric direction and horizon.
Black bold marks the lowest error or highest success point mean within that
group, including a baseline when it wins. Green bold marks a positive gain
against the explicitly named reference; neither style establishes statistical
significance. Paired intervals remain in the accompanying inference tables.
The proposed method is labeled ShiftWM (ours); removing a component defines an
ablation, not a separately proposed algorithm. An exploratory development winner
does not silently replace the selected model or its earlier reported results.

\paragraph{Additional measurements and completion rules.}
Physical goal error and actions to success complement simulator success; existing
task traces do not yet supply every comparable aggregate. A shared efficiency
panel should measure encoder-inclusive and predictor-only latency, complete CEM
replanning cost where applicable, peak memory, active parameters and training
GPU-hours under matched hardware and precision. These are pending measurements,
not zero-valued results. RGB/video scores require a separately evaluated decoder;
they cannot be inferred from feature errors. New numerical cells and plots are
populated from completed, source-bound comparisons. Ongoing single-seed diagnostics
do not bypass the full-campaign reporting gate. All unfavorable results and
prespecified conditions remain visible.
