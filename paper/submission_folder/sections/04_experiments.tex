\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

We ask four questions. \textbf{(Q1)} Does transport improve held-out forecasting over matched
predictors on real robot and surgical video? \textbf{(Q2)} Does the gain hold across encoders and
horizons? \textbf{(Q3)} What happens in closed-loop planning? \textbf{(Q4)} What does the transport
field learn? All numbers come from completed runs with three training seeds. Model and checkpoint
selection use validation splits only, and test splits are scored once.

\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
\paragraph{Datasets.}
\textbf{DROID} \citep{khazatsky2024droid}: 1{,}126 real Franka episodes from 24 official shards,
split by recording session (851/143/132 train/val/test). We use the primary exterior camera and
report the second exterior camera as a transfer view. One model step is 5 frames (0.33\,s) with a
35-D block of Cartesian and gripper commands, and we forecast up to $K{=}10$ steps (3.3\,s).
\textbf{Open-H-Embodiment, Hamlyn dVRK} \citep{openh2026embodiment}: seven surgical tasks (suturing
$\times$2, knot tying, needle grasp and handover, peg transfer, tissue lifting, tissue retraction)
with endoscope video and recorded robot kinematics as actions. Splits are episode-disjoint per task
(70/15/15). \textbf{IWS} \citep{wang2026iws,zhang2026rlawm}: single-image forecasting on PushT, Box
and Rope with the official RLA-WM evaluation handles. \textbf{Planning}: PushT, TwoRoom and Reacher
from the LeWM/stable-worldmodel suite \citep{maes2026lewm,maes2026stable}, with official planner
budgets.

\paragraph{Encoders and models.} Frozen DINOv2-S/14 and DINOv2-B/14 \citep{oquab2023dinov2}
($16\times16$ patches at 224\,px) and DINOv3-S/16 \citep{simeoni2025dinov3} ($16\times16$ at
256\,px). No pooling is applied. All learned predictors use width 384 with $L_e{=}L_d{=}4$
($\approx$\pend{}M parameters) and the same optimiser, schedule, number of steps and batch size
(\cref{app:impl}).

\paragraph{Metrics.} Forecasting: standardised feature MSE per horizon (mean over horizons and at
the final horizon), cosine similarity, and MSE after $4{\times}4$ pooling for comparison with prior
work. Decoded-pixel: PSNR, SSIM and LPIPS through a shared feature-to-RGB decoder. Physical
grounding: linear probes of end-effector or kinematic state from predicted features. Action
sensitivity: error under shuffled actions and the rate at which true actions rank above shuffled
ones. Planning: success rate and planning time. Statistics: episodes are the unit of analysis, and we
report paired bootstrap 95\% intervals over episodes, pooled across seeds.
