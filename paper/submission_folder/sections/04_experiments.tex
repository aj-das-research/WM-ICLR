\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

We ask four questions. \textbf{(Q1)} Does transport improve held-out forecasting over matched predictors on real
robot and surgical video (\cref{sec:forecasting})? \textbf{(Q2)} Does the \ours{} head improve strong
\emph{published} world models when plugged into them (\cref{sec:plugin})? \textbf{(Q3)} Which components matter
(\cref{sec:ablations}), and what happens in closed-loop planning (\cref{sec:planning})? \textbf{(Q4)} Why does it
work (\cref{sec:analysis})? Selection uses validation splits only; test splits are scored once.

\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
\paragraph{Datasets} (\cref{tab:datasets}).
\textbf{DROID} \citep{khazatsky2024droid}: 1{,}126 real Franka episodes from 24 official shards, split by recording
session (851/143/132). We train on the primary exterior camera and evaluate transfer to the second exterior camera.
One model step is 5 frames (0.33\,s) with a 35-D block of Cartesian and gripper commands; we forecast $K{=}10$ steps
(3.3\,s). \textbf{Open-H-Embodiment, Hamlyn dVRK} \citep{openh2026embodiment}: 1{,}013 episodes of seven surgical
training tasks, 80-D bimanual Cartesian action blocks, episode-disjoint splits per task (708/152/153).
\textbf{IWS} \citep{wang2026iws,zhang2026rlawm}: single-image forecasting on PushT, Box and Rope with the official
RLA-WM handles. \textbf{BridgeData V2} \citep{walke2023bridgedata} and \textbf{RT-1 (Fractal)} \citep{oneill2024openx}:
pinned Open-X subsets (734 and 10{,}700 episodes). \textbf{Language-Table} \citep{lynch2023interactive}: an xArm pushing rigid, coloured blocks (Open-X subset, 2{,}589 episodes, 2-D actions). \textbf{Planning}: PushT, TwoRoom and Reacher with the official
LeWM/stable-worldmodel protocol \citep{maes2026lewm,maes2026stable}; PushT and Wall with the official DINO-WM protocol.

\paragraph{Models and metrics.} All learned predictors share a frozen DINOv2-S/14 encoder ($16{\times}16$ patches, no
pooling), width 384 and $L_e{=}L_d{=}4$ ($\approx$22M parameters), and the same optimiser, schedule, steps and batch
size (\cref{app:impl}); they differ only in the output head and rollout. The primary metric is standardised feature MSE
on held-out episodes, averaged over horizons and at the final step. We also report \emph{skill}, the fraction of
persistence error a model removes; being scale-free, it compares models in different feature spaces. Episodes are the
statistical unit; we report means over seeds and paired session-bootstrap 95\% intervals (\cref{app:stats}).

\Cref{tab:main} compares \ours{} with predictors that share its backbone and differ only in how the forecast is formed.
On held-out DROID, \ours{} achieves the lowest error of all methods: it removes \droidSkill\% of the persistence error,
against \droidSkillDirect\% for Direct and \droidSkillAR\% for AR, i.e.\ \droidVsDirect\% lower error than Direct,
\droidVsAR\% lower than the rollout-trained AR and \droidVsARTF\% lower than the DINO-WM-style AR-TF, whose teacher-forced
absolute predictions drift below persistence once rolled out. The same ordering holds on the seven surgical tasks
of Open-H, where \ours{} is \hamlynVsDirect\% below Direct and \hamlynVsAR\% below AR. \Cref{fig:horizon} resolves the
error by horizon: recursive predictors accumulate error as they consume their own forecasts
(Proposition~\ref{prop:compound}), while \ours{} stays lowest at every step.

\subsection{\ours{} as a plug-in for published world models (Q2)}
\label{sec:plugin}
\input{submission_folder/tables/plugin.tex}
Because \ours{} is an output head, we attach it to two published models without other changes: it blends the backbone's
prediction $\mathbf{P}$ with transported observed features, $\hat{\Z}=(1-g)\mathbf{P}+g\,\mathbf{T}$, with the gate
initialised near zero so training starts from the unmodified model. Each pair shares data, schedule, loss and seed.

\paragraph{V-JEPA 2-AC.} The released model \citep{assran2025vjepa2} (ViT-g encoder, 300M predictor, trained on DROID)
removes \vjepaSkillZS\% of persistence error zero-shot on our test windows, \vjepaSkillFT\% after fine-tuning, and
\good{\vjepaSkillOurs\%} after fine-tuning \emph{with} the head under the identical budget (\cref{tab:plugin}):
\vjepaMSERed\% lower error and \vjepaMovingRed\% lower on moving patches for 0.16M added parameters; the gate opens to
transport on most patches (\cref{fig:vjepa-plugin}).

\paragraph{DINO-WM.} With the official code \citep{zhou2025dinowm}, the head lowers latent error by
\good{\dinowmPushtErrRed\%} on PushT (0.263 $\to$ 0.235 over a 5-step rollout) but \emph{raises} it by
\dinowmWallErrInc\% on Wall, where DINO-WM conditions on a single history frame. Planning success changes from
\dinowmPushtSuccBase\% to \dinowmPushtSuccOurs\% (PushT) and \dinowmWallSuccBase\% to \dinowmWallSuccOurs\% (Wall);
budgets are reduced and identical for both arms (\cref{app:baselines}).
