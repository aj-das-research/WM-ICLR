\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

We ask four questions. \textbf{(Q1)} Does transport improve held-out forecasting over matched
predictors on real robot and surgical video (\cref{sec:forecasting})? \textbf{(Q2)} Which components
matter, and does the gain hold across encoders and horizons (\cref{sec:ablations})?
\textbf{(Q3)} What happens in closed-loop planning (\cref{sec:planning})? \textbf{(Q4)} What does the
transport field learn (\cref{sec:analysis})? All numbers come from completed runs with three
training seeds. Selection uses validation splits only, and test splits are scored once.

\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
\paragraph{Datasets} (\cref{tab:datasets}).
\textbf{DROID} \citep{khazatsky2024droid}: 1{,}126 real Franka episodes from 24 official shards,
split by recording session (851/143/132). We train on the primary exterior camera and evaluate
transfer to the second exterior camera. One model step is 5 frames (0.33\,s) with a 35-D block of
Cartesian and gripper commands, and we forecast $K{=}10$ steps (3.3\,s).
\textbf{Open-H-Embodiment, Hamlyn dVRK} \citep{openh2026embodiment}: 1{,}013 episodes of seven surgical
training tasks recorded with a scene camera. Actions are 80-D blocks of bimanual Cartesian targets,
and one step is 1/3\,s. Splits are episode-disjoint per task (708/152/153).
\textbf{IWS} \citep{wang2026iws,zhang2026rlawm}: single-image forecasting on PushT, Box and Rope,
evaluated on the official RLA-WM handles. \textbf{Planning}: PushT, TwoRoom and Reacher with the
official LeWM/stable-worldmodel protocol \citep{maes2026lewm,maes2026stable}.

\paragraph{Encoders, models and metrics.} We use frozen DINOv2-S/14 and DINOv2-B/14
\citep{oquab2023dinov2} and DINOv3-S/16 \citep{simeoni2025dinov3}, all at $16\times16$ patches with no
pooling. Every learned predictor has width 384 and $L_e{=}L_d{=}4$ ($\approx$22M parameters), and
all share the optimiser, schedule, step count and batch size (\cref{app:impl}). Forecasting metrics
are standardised feature MSE (averaged over horizons, and at the final horizon), cosine similarity,
and MSE after $4{\times}4$ pooling. We also report decoded PSNR, SSIM and LPIPS, linear probes of
robot state, and action sensitivity. For planning we report success rate and planning time.
Episodes are the statistical unit, and we give paired bootstrap 95\% intervals (\cref{app:stats}).
