\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
Code, configurations, per-episode evaluation records and trained checkpoints will be released; the model is
also packaged as an installable library (\cref{app:software}).
\Cref{app:impl} lists every hyperparameter. Dataset splits are deterministic and documented in
\cref{app:data}. All datasets are public, namely DROID (CC-BY-4.0), Open-H-Embodiment (CC-BY-4.0), Language-Table via Open-X (CC-BY-4.0), the IWS recordings of the RLA-WM release (no licence stated; used for research as released) and the DINO-WM
releases.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}
The surgical data are public recordings of robotic procedures on table-top phantoms and ex-vivo porcine tissue,
released under CC-BY-4.0. We make no clinical claims, and the models are not intended for clinical
use.
