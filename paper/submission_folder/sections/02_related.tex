\paragraph{Latent world models.}
Predicting dynamics in the embedding space of a frozen encoder keeps world models small enough to plan with: DINO-WM
predicts DINOv2 patch features for model-predictive control~\citep{zhou2025dinowm,oquab2023dinov2}, V-JEPA~2-AC
post-trains an action-conditioned predictor on DROID~\citep{assran2025vjepa2,khazatsky2024droid}, and joint-embedding
and foundation-feature forecasters extend this setting~\citep{sobal2025pldm,maes2026lewm,balestriero2025lejepa,terver2026jepawms,baldassarre2025dinoworld,karypidis2025dinoforesight,boduljak2025vfmf,porcher2026flowwm}.
%
DDP-WM separates dynamic regions from a static background, RLA-WM models residual latent actions
\citep{yin2026ddpwm,zhang2026rlawm}, and recent analyses study which latent spaces make predictions action-relevant
\citep{nilaksh2026reconstruction,yeom2026actionrelevant,zhang2026deltajepa,huang2026nanowm}; pixel-space and model-based
reinforcement-learning world models are complementary \citep{bar2025nwm,nvidia2025cosmos,hafner2025dreamerv3,hansen2024tdmpc2}.
%
All these predictors synthesise the future feature maps, including content that only moves, whereas \ours{} forms each
forecast as a corrected transport of observed features and attaches to them as a head.

\paragraph{Multi-step prediction.}
Iterating a one-step model compounds its errors~\citep{venkatraman2015multistep}, yet most latent world models are rolled
out recursively after teacher-forced training, sometimes with a rollout loss~\citep{zhou2025dinowm,assran2025vjepa2};
latent overshooting~\citep{hafner2019planet}, anchor frames~\citep{luan2026anchorednwm} and decision-oriented
objectives~\citep{wang2026dalewm} reduce the resulting drift.
%
Direct prediction of every horizon avoids feedback but still re-synthesises static and moving content, which \ours{}
keeps or moves instead.

\paragraph{Motion and feature warping.}
Moving existing content has a long history in pixel prediction, with action-conditioned kernels~\citep{finn2016advection},
dynamic filters~\citep{debrabandere2016dfn}, adversarial transformations~\citep{vondrick2017transformers}, skip
connections~\citep{ebert2017skip}, resampling along predicted displacements~\citep{reda2018sdcnet,liu2017voxelflow} and
stochastic or diffusion variants~\citep{lee2018savp,voleti2022mcvd}.
%
In feature space, features are propagated along optical flow~\citep{zhu2017dff}, forecast for
segmentation~\citep{luc2017deeper,luc2018f2f}, warped along a forecast motion field and blended with a regression
(F2M;~\citealp{saric2020warp}), or copied forward by attention~\citep{oh2019stm}, and recent robot world models add 3D
scene flow, optical-flow velocity or object slots to condition a generator
\citep{guo2026flowdreamer,syed2026ahead,wu2023slotformer,mosbach2025sold}.
%
\ours{} carries move-and-correct into latent world models: unlike F2M, it learns an action-conditioned soft transport
over a local window of several observed frames from the forecasting loss alone, gates it against an exact identity
path, decodes every horizon in parallel and attaches as a plug-in head, and it has lower error than F2M-style warping
with optical flow (\cref{sec:analysis}).
