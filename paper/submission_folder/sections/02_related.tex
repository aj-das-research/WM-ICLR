\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Latent world models.}
Predicting dynamics in the embedding space of an encoder rather than in pixels keeps world models small enough to plan
with.
%
DINO-WM trains a causal ViT predictor on frozen DINOv2 patch features and plans with model-predictive
control~\citep{zhou2025dinowm,oquab2023dinov2}, and V-JEPA~2-AC post-trains an action-conditioned predictor on DROID for
zero-shot robot planning~\citep{assran2025vjepa2,khazatsky2024droid}.
%
PLDM, LeWM and JEPA-WMs learn joint-embedding predictive models from offline
trajectories~\citep{sobal2025pldm,maes2026lewm,balestriero2025lejepa,terver2026jepawms}, and DINO-world,
DINO-Foresight, VFMF and FlowWM forecast future foundation features, deterministically or
generatively~\citep{baldassarre2025dinoworld,karypidis2025dinoforesight,boduljak2025vfmf,porcher2026flowwm}.
%
DDP-WM separates sparse dynamic regions from a static background, RLA-WM models residual latent
actions~\citep{yin2026ddpwm,zhang2026rlawm}, and recent analyses ask which latent spaces and objectives make predictions
action-relevant~\citep{nilaksh2026reconstruction,yeom2026actionrelevant,zhang2026deltajepa,huang2026nanowm}.
%
Pixel-space and model-based reinforcement-learning world models are complementary
\citep{bar2025nwm,nvidia2025cosmos,hafner2025dreamerv3,hansen2024tdmpc2}.
%
All these predictors synthesise the future feature maps, including content that only moves, whereas \ours{} keeps
their frozen-encoder interface, forms each forecast as a corrected transport of observed features and attaches to them
as a head.

\paragraph{Multi-step prediction.}
Iterating a one-step model compounds its errors~\citep{venkatraman2015multistep}, yet most latent world models are
trained for one step and rolled out recursively.
%
DINO-WM uses teacher forcing~\citep{zhou2025dinowm}, and V-JEPA~2-AC adds a rollout loss to teacher-forced
training~\citep{assran2025vjepa2}.
%
PlaNet's latent overshooting reduces the mismatch between one-step training and multi-step use~\citep{hafner2019planet},
anchored navigation world models resist drift by conditioning on anchor frames~\citep{luan2026anchorednwm}, and
decision-oriented objectives align latent predictions with the costs a planner ranks~\citep{wang2026dalewm}.
%
Direct prediction of every horizon avoids feedback, but a direct regressor still re-synthesises static and moving
content, which \ours{} copies instead.

\paragraph{Motion and feature warping.}
Predicting the future by moving existing content has a long history in pixel space.
%
CDNA and DNA transform the previous frame with action-conditioned kernels~\citep{finn2016advection}, dynamic filter
networks generate input-dependent filters~\citep{debrabandere2016dfn}, and \citet{vondrick2017transformers} transform
past pixels adversarially.
%
SNA adds skip connections to the first frame~\citep{ebert2017skip}, SDC-Net and voxel flow resample past pixels along
predicted displacements~\citep{reda2018sdcnet,liu2017voxelflow}, and SAVP and MCVD add stochastic and diffusion
models~\citep{lee2018savp,voleti2022mcvd}.
%
In feature space, Deep Feature Flow propagates CNN features along optical flow~\citep{zhu2017dff}, F2F forecasts future
features for segmentation~\citep{luc2017deeper,luc2018f2f}, F2M warps observed features with a forecast motion field
and blends them with a regressed forecast~\citep{saric2020warp}, and space-time memory networks copy past features
forward by attention~\citep{oh2019stm}.
%
Recent robot world models reintroduce structure through 3D scene flow (FlowDreamer;~\citealp{guo2026flowdreamer}),
optical-flow velocity (AHEAD;~\citealp{syed2026ahead}) or object slots~\citep{wu2023slotformer,mosbach2025sold}.
%
The pixel methods move pixels, the feature methods move features without actions, and the robot world models use
structure to condition a generator rather than as the forecast itself.
%
\ours{} carries move-and-correct into latent world models as an action-conditioned transport of frozen foundation-model
features, decoded in parallel for every horizon and usable as a plug-in head.
