\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models in foundation-feature and JEPA latent spaces.}
A recent line of work predicts dynamics in the embedding space of a pretrained or jointly learned encoder instead of in pixels. DINO-WM trains a causal ViT predictor on frozen DINOv2 patch features and plans with model-predictive control~\citep{zhou2025dinowm,oquab2023dinov2}; PLDM, LeWM and JEPA-WMs study joint-embedding predictive models learned from offline trajectories~\citep{sobal2025pldm,maes2026lewm,balestriero2025lejepa,terver2026jepawms}, and V-JEPA~2-AC post-trains an action-conditioned predictor on DROID for zero-shot robot planning~\citep{assran2025vjepa2,khazatsky2024droid}. DINO-world, DINO-Foresight, VFMF and FlowWM forecast future foundation features, deterministically or generatively, and evaluate them through downstream dense heads~\citep{baldassarre2025dinoworld,karypidis2025dinoforesight,boduljak2025vfmf,porcher2026flowwm}. DDP-WM separates sparse dynamic regions from a static background, and RLA-WM models residual latent actions over visual features~\citep{yin2026ddpwm,zhang2026rlawm}. Complementary analyses ask which latent spaces and objectives make predictions action-relevant~\citep{nilaksh2026reconstruction,yeom2026actionrelevant,zhang2026deltajepa,huang2026nanowm}. In these models the predictor synthesizes each future feature map and, in most cases, conditions later steps on its own outputs. ShiftWM keeps the same frozen-encoder interface, applicable to DINOv2 or DINOv3 features~\citep{simeoni2025dinov3}, but changes the output parameterization: every forecast is expressed as a transport of observed features plus a correction, so the predictor is never required to regenerate static content.

\paragraph{Generative world models and model-based reinforcement learning.}
Pixel-space generative world models learn high-fidelity, action-controllable video simulators. NWM conditions a diffusion transformer on navigation actions~\citep{bar2025nwm}; Cosmos and Cosmos-Predict2.5 provide large video foundation models for physical AI~\citep{nvidia2025cosmos,nvidia2025cosmospredict25} that Cosmos Policy adapts for control~\citep{kim2026cosmospolicy}; Ctrl-World, IRASim, UniSim and IWS target robot manipulation and policy evaluation~\citep{guo2026ctrlworld,zhu2025irasim,yang2024unisim,wang2026iws}; and the Genie family generates interactive environments from video, image or text prompts~\citep{bruce2024genie,parkerholder2025genie3}. Model-based reinforcement learning instead learns task-specific latent dynamics jointly with rewards and policies, from PlaNet and DreamerV3 to TD-MPC2, IRIS and DIAMOND~\citep{hafner2019planet,hafner2025dreamerv3,hansen2024tdmpc2,micheli2023iris,alonso2024diamond}. These systems optimize sample fidelity or online return with their own encoders, data and, often, billions of parameters, so they do not share our contract of a fixed frozen encoder, identical observations and actions, and targets in common feature coordinates. We therefore treat them as complementary rather than as compute-matched baselines: they address rendering fidelity and online control, whereas we isolate the output parameterization of a compact predictor on a fixed representation.

\paragraph{Transformation- and transport-based prediction.}
Predicting the future by moving existing content has a long history in video prediction. CDNA and DNA predict action-conditioned convolutional kernels or pixel-motion distributions that transform the previous frame~\citep{finn2016advection}, dynamic filter networks generate input-dependent filters~\citep{debrabandere2016dfn}, and Vondrick and Torralba transform past pixels with adversarially trained transformers~\citep{vondrick2017transformers}. SNA adds temporal skip connections to the first observed image to handle occlusion~\citep{ebert2017skip}, while flow-warping approaches synthesize frames by resampling along predicted voxel flow~\citep{liu2017voxelflow}; SAVP and MCVD extend pixel prediction with stochastic and diffusion models~\citep{lee2018savp,voleti2022mcvd}. Recent robot world models reintroduce explicit motion: FlowDreamer predicts 3D scene flow before diffusion rendering~\citep{guo2026flowdreamer}, AHEAD forecasts VLA patch tokens conditioned on optical-flow velocity~\citep{syed2026ahead}, and anchored navigation world models resist drift through anchor frames and epipolar guidance~\citep{luan2026anchorednwm}. Object-centric models such as SlotFormer and SOLD impose structure through slots~\citep{wu2023slotformer,mosbach2025sold}. ShiftWM differs from these designs in four respects. It transports frozen foundation features rather than pixels; its source is a fixed memory of \emph{observed} feature grids that remains unchanged at every horizon, so predicted states are never fed back as inputs; each horizon-$h$ forecast is produced directly from the action prefix $a_{1:h}$; and the transport is a local softmax over a spatial neighborhood, combined with a gated identity path and a learned correction for disocclusion and appearance change.

\paragraph{World models for surgery and evaluation suites.}
Surgical world modeling is driven by generative video models: Endora simulates endoscopy video~\citep{li2024endora}, SurgWM learns latent-action-controllable surgical video~\citep{koju2025surgwm}, SurgVista targets long-horizon instrument--tissue dynamics~\citep{pan2026surgvista}, SWoMo combines neural and symbolic components for cataract surgery~\citep{sivakumar2026swomo}, and Cosmos-H-Surgical learns surgical robot policies from videos through world modeling~\citep{he2025cosmoshsurgical}. Open-H-Embodiment releases paired video and kinematics across surgical platforms, together with a kinematics-conditioned surgical simulator~\citep{openh2026embodiment}; SutureBot, JIGSAWS, Cholec80 and CholecT50 provide surgical demonstrations and task, skill and workflow annotations~\citep{haworth2025suturebot,gao2014jigsaws,twinanda2017endonet,nwoye2022rendezvous}, and SurgWMBench benchmarks instrument-motion planning~\citep{liu2026surgwmbench}. We use Open-H dVRK recordings to test kinematics-conditioned forecasting in feature space, a regime these pixel generators do not target. More broadly, robot datasets such as DROID, BridgeData~V2 and Open X-Embodiment supply action-labeled video~\citep{khazatsky2024droid,walke2023bridgedata,oneill2024openx}. WorldScore, WorldModelBench, EWMBench and Physics-IQ score generated video for controllability and physical plausibility~\citep{duan2025worldscore,li2025worldmodelbench,yue2025ewmbench,motamed2026physicsiq}, whereas stable-worldmodel standardizes latent planning evaluation~\citep{maes2026stable}. We follow the latter protocols for control and report feature-space forecasting error for prediction.
