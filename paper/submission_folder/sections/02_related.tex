\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Latent and foundation-feature world models.}
A recent line of work predicts dynamics in the embedding space of a pretrained or jointly learned encoder instead of in
pixels. DINO-WM trains a causal ViT predictor on frozen DINOv2 patch features and plans with model-predictive
control~\citep{zhou2025dinowm,oquab2023dinov2}; PLDM, LeWM and JEPA-WMs learn joint-embedding predictive models from
offline trajectories~\citep{sobal2025pldm,maes2026lewm,balestriero2025lejepa,terver2026jepawms}; and V-JEPA~2-AC
post-trains an action-conditioned predictor on DROID for zero-shot robot planning~\citep{assran2025vjepa2,khazatsky2024droid}.
DINO-world, DINO-Foresight, VFMF and FlowWM forecast future foundation features, deterministically or
generatively~\citep{baldassarre2025dinoworld,karypidis2025dinoforesight,boduljak2025vfmf,porcher2026flowwm}; DDP-WM
separates sparse dynamic regions from a static background, RLA-WM models residual latent actions~\citep{yin2026ddpwm,zhang2026rlawm},
and several analyses ask which latent spaces and objectives make predictions action-relevant~\citep{nilaksh2026reconstruction,yeom2026actionrelevant,zhang2026deltajepa,huang2026nanowm}.
Pixel-space generative world models and model-based reinforcement learning address rendering fidelity and online
control with their own encoders, data and training budgets~\citep{bar2025nwm,nvidia2025cosmos,hafner2025dreamerv3,hansen2024tdmpc2};
we treat them as complementary (\cref{app:related-extended}). In all latent models above, the predictor synthesises
every future feature map, including content that only moves. \ours{} keeps the frozen-encoder interface but changes the
output parameterisation: each forecast is a transport of observed features plus a correction, and the same head can be
attached to these models.

\paragraph{Recursive versus direct multi-step prediction.}
Iterating a one-step model compounds its errors, a classic observation in multi-step learning~\citep{venkatraman2015multistep}.
Most latent world models are nevertheless trained for one step and rolled out recursively. DINO-WM uses teacher forcing and
autoregressive rollouts~\citep{zhou2025dinowm}, V-JEPA~2-AC adds a rollout loss to teacher-forced training~\citep{assran2025vjepa2},
and PlaNet introduced multi-step latent overshooting to reduce the mismatch between one-step training and multi-step
use~\citep{hafner2019planet}. Anchored navigation world models resist drift by conditioning on anchor frames~\citep{luan2026anchorednwm},
and decision-oriented objectives align latent predictions with the costs that a planner ranks~\citep{wang2026dalewm}.
Direct prediction of every horizon avoids feedback, but a direct regressor must still regenerate the whole grid, and
anchoring to the present makes it easy to ignore actions. \ours{}, like our Direct baseline, decodes every horizon from
measured features; it differs in \emph{what} it decodes, and it adds an action-contrastive objective to keep anchored
forecasts sensitive to the actions a planner compares.

\paragraph{Motion-, flow- and feature-warping prediction.}
Predicting the future by moving existing content has a long history. In pixel space, CDNA and DNA transform the previous
frame with action-conditioned kernels or motion distributions~\citep{finn2016advection}, dynamic filter networks
generate input-dependent filters~\citep{debrabandere2016dfn}, Vondrick and Torralba transform past pixels with
adversarially trained transformers~\citep{vondrick2017transformers}, SNA adds skip connections to the first frame to
handle occlusion~\citep{ebert2017skip}, and voxel flow resamples frames along predicted flow~\citep{liu2017voxelflow};
SAVP and MCVD add stochastic and diffusion models~\citep{lee2018savp,voleti2022mcvd}. In feature space, Deep Feature Flow
propagates CNN features along optical flow instead of recomputing them~\citep{zhu2017dff}, F2F forecasts future
convolutional features for semantic and instance segmentation~\citep{luc2017deeper,luc2018f2f}, and F2M warps observed
features with a forecast feature-motion field and blends them with a directly regressed forecast~\citep{saric2020warp}.
Recent robot world models reintroduce explicit motion: FlowDreamer predicts 3D scene flow before diffusion
rendering~\citep{guo2026flowdreamer}, AHEAD conditions token forecasts on optical-flow velocity~\citep{syed2026ahead},
and object-centric models impose structure through slots~\citep{wu2023slotformer,mosbach2025sold}. Feature-warping
forecasters are not action-conditioned and serve dense recognition, while robot models warp pixels or flow. \ours{}
carries the warp-and-correct idea into latent world models: a learned soft local transport of frozen foundation-model
patch features, conditioned on the action prefix, gated against the identity with a correction, decoded in parallel for
every horizon, and usable as a plug-in head for published world models.
