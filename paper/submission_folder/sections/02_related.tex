\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Latent world models.}
A recent line of work predicts dynamics in the embedding space of a pretrained or jointly learned encoder rather than
pixels. DINO-WM trains a causal ViT predictor on frozen DINOv2 patch features and plans with model-predictive
control~\citep{zhou2025dinowm,oquab2023dinov2}; PLDM, LeWM and JEPA-WMs learn joint-embedding predictive models from
offline trajectories~\citep{sobal2025pldm,maes2026lewm,balestriero2025lejepa,terver2026jepawms}; and V-JEPA~2-AC
post-trains an action-conditioned predictor on DROID for zero-shot robot planning~\citep{assran2025vjepa2,khazatsky2024droid}.
DINO-world, DINO-Foresight, VFMF and FlowWM forecast future foundation features, deterministically or
generatively~\citep{baldassarre2025dinoworld,karypidis2025dinoforesight,boduljak2025vfmf,porcher2026flowwm}; DDP-WM
separates sparse dynamic regions from a static background, RLA-WM models residual latent actions~\citep{yin2026ddpwm,zhang2026rlawm},
and analyses ask which latent spaces and objectives make predictions action-relevant~\citep{nilaksh2026reconstruction,yeom2026actionrelevant,zhang2026deltajepa,huang2026nanowm}.
Pixel-space and model-based RL world models are complementary~\citep{bar2025nwm,nvidia2025cosmos,hafner2025dreamerv3,hansen2024tdmpc2} (\cref{app:related-extended}).
These predictors synthesise the future feature maps, including content that only moves. \ours{} keeps their
frozen-encoder interface but forms each forecast as a corrected transport of observed features, and attaches to them as a head.

\paragraph{Multi-step prediction.}
Iterating a one-step model compounds its errors~\citep{venkatraman2015multistep}, yet most latent world models are
trained for one step and rolled out recursively: DINO-WM uses teacher forcing~\citep{zhou2025dinowm}, and V-JEPA~2-AC
adds a rollout loss to teacher-forced training~\citep{assran2025vjepa2}. PlaNet's latent overshooting reduces the mismatch
between one-step training and multi-step use~\citep{hafner2019planet}, anchored navigation world models resist drift by
conditioning on anchor frames~\citep{luan2026anchorednwm}, and decision-oriented objectives align latent predictions with
the costs a planner ranks~\citep{wang2026dalewm}. Direct prediction of every horizon avoids feedback, but a direct
regressor still re-synthesises static and moving content. \ours{} decodes every horizon directly and copies that content
instead of regenerating it.

\paragraph{Motion and feature warping.}
Predicting the future by moving existing content has a long history in pixel space: CDNA and DNA transform the previous
frame with action-conditioned kernels~\citep{finn2016advection}, dynamic filter networks generate input-dependent
filters~\citep{debrabandere2016dfn}, \citet{vondrick2017transformers} transform past pixels adversarially, SNA adds skip
connections to the first frame~\citep{ebert2017skip}, SDC-Net samples past pixels with predicted displacements and
kernels~\citep{reda2018sdcnet}, voxel flow resamples frames along predicted flow~\citep{liu2017voxelflow}, and SAVP and
MCVD add stochastic and diffusion models~\citep{lee2018savp,voleti2022mcvd}. In feature space, Deep Feature Flow
propagates CNN features along optical flow~\citep{zhu2017dff}, F2F forecasts future features for
segmentation~\citep{luc2017deeper,luc2018f2f}, F2M warps observed features with a forecast feature-motion field and blends
them with a regressed forecast~\citep{saric2020warp}, and space-time memory networks copy past features forward by
attention~\citep{oh2019stm}. Recent robot world models reintroduce structure through 3D scene flow
(FlowDreamer;~\citealp{guo2026flowdreamer}), optical-flow velocity (AHEAD;~\citealp{syed2026ahead}) or object
slots~\citep{wu2023slotformer,mosbach2025sold}. The pixel methods move \emph{pixels}, the feature methods move features
without actions, and the robot world models use structure to condition a generator rather than as the forecast itself. \ours{} carries move-and-correct into latent world models: an action-conditioned transport of
frozen foundation-model features, decoded in parallel for every horizon and usable as a plug-in head.
