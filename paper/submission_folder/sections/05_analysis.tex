\paragraph{How does \ours{} compose a forecast?} \Cref{fig:mechanism}(a) projects the $16{\times}16{\times}C$ feature grids
to colour with a shared PCA: the \ours{} forecast preserves the layout of the true future, while Direct and AR drift
toward a smoother grid. Because \cref{eq:output} is additive, each forecast splits exactly into the kept term
$(1-g)\Z_0$, the moved term $g\mathbf{T}$ and the correction $\mathbf{r}$ (\cref{fig:mechanism}b): the static scene
is carried by the kept term, the moving arm by transport, and the correction concentrates on newly revealed content.
\Cref{fig:mechanism}(c) quantifies this over all DROID test windows as the spatial variance of the forecast relative
to the true grid. Regression toward the conditional mean loses contrast as the horizon grows; transport copies measured
features and keeps more of it (0.80 vs.\ 0.77 for Direct and 0.74 for AR at $k{=}10$).

\paragraph{What does the transport field learn? (Q4)} We convert $\pi_{k,i}$ into an expected source
displacement per patch and compare it, at patch resolution, with optical flow estimated by RAFT
\citep{teed2020raft} between the observed and future frames (\cref{fig:qualitative}). In \cref{fig:qualitative}, transport concentrates
on the moving arm, gripper and instruments; the static background keeps the identity path, and newly revealed
regions fall back to the correction term. \pend{}

\paragraph{Where do the gains come from?} We bin test patches by the magnitude of their true feature
change and compare the per-bin error of \ours{} and Direct (\cref{app:analysis}). \pend{}

\paragraph{Accuracy versus action sensitivity.} A forecast that stays close to $\Z_0$ can score
well on average while barely responding to the commanded actions, and a planner then cannot rank
candidate action sequences. We measure this directly as the rate at which true future actions yield
lower error than actions from another episode (\cref{fig:tradeoff}, left). Anchored predictors can reach low
open-loop error while reacting weakly to actions; the action-contrastive objective moves \ours{} toward high
accuracy \emph{and} high sensitivity. \pend{}
