\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\ours{} forecasts by moving what it has already seen, plus a small correction. It is the most accurate predictor on
held-out robot and surgical video, improves published world models as a plug-in head, and makes predicted motion explicit.
\textbf{Limitations.} Pixel metrics rely on a separate decoder; newly revealed content depends on the correction; the
surgical data are scene-camera views of dVRK training tasks, not in-vivo endoscopy.
