\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented \ours{}, a latent world model that forecasts by moving observed foundation features:
for every future step, a gated local transport of measured patch features plus a learned correction.
Across held-out real robot video, surgical robot video, single-image manipulation forecasting and
planning benchmarks, \pend{}. The transport field gives a readable view of the predicted motion, and
our analysis of action sensitivity shows what anchoring costs and how to recover it for control.

\paragraph{Limitations.} \ours{} predicts features, not pixels, so visualisations and pixel metrics
rely on a separately trained decoder. Transport can only copy content that has been observed, so
newly revealed regions depend on the correction term. The action-contrastive objective uses in-batch
negatives, which can be easy to tell apart in diverse datasets. The surgical benchmark uses scene-camera
views of dVRK training tasks, not in-vivo endoscopy.
