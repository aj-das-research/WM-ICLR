\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented \ours{}, a latent world model that forecasts by transporting observed foundation
features and synthesising only what transport cannot explain. \pend{}

\paragraph{Limitations.} \ours{} predicts features, not pixels. Visualisations and pixel metrics
rely on a separately trained decoder. Transport copies only content that has been observed, so newly
revealed regions depend on the correction term. The action-contrastive objective uses in-batch
negatives, which may be easy to distinguish in diverse datasets.
