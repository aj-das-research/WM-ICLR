\paragraph{Limitations.} The plug-in does not help uniformly: on DINO-WM Wall, with a single history frame, it raises
latent error. Gains on the surgical tasks are small, and arm placement improves only marginally over Direct.
Newly revealed content depends on the correction, the forecast is deterministic, pixel metrics rely on a separate
decoder, and the surgical data are scene-camera views of dVRK training tasks, not in-vivo endoscopy.
