\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\ours{} forecasts by moving what it has already seen and correcting only what is new.
%
Against matched predictors, it has the lowest held-out error on DROID (including an unseen camera), Language-Table, three
bimanual IWS tasks and seven surgical tasks, and it wins on \wmWinDirect\% of DROID test episodes.
%
As a plug-in head, it lowers the error of the 1.3B-parameter V-JEPA 2-AC by \vjepaMSERed\% with 0.16M added parameters.
%
Move-and-correct is a simple and general output parameterisation, and we encourage latent world models to form their
forecasts from what they have observed.

\paragraph{Limitations.} Content that no observed frame contains, such as newly exposed background or an object
entering the view, cannot be moved and must come from the correction: even the oracle move removes only
\geoOracleRed\% of the persistence error on moving content (\cref{fig:evidence}c), and for the rest \ours{} falls back to regression,
like its baselines.
%
Its forecasts are also deterministic: under uncertain motion the MSE-optimal forecast averages the plausible sources
(Proposition~\ref{prop:bayes}), which stochastic feature forecasters \citep{boduljak2025vfmf,porcher2026flowwm} could
complement.
%
Two properties of the data bound the evaluation (\cref{app:data}): Language-Table segments may share a play session
across splits, and the Hamlyn videos are external-camera views of practice tasks, not clinical endoscopy.
