\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\ours{} forecasts by moving what it has already seen and correcting only what is new. Against matched predictors it has
the lowest held-out error on real-robot video, on an unseen camera and on all seven surgical tasks, and it wins on
\wmWinDirect\% of DROID test episodes; as a plug-in head it lowers the error of the 1.3B-parameter V-JEPA 2-AC by
\vjepaMSERed\%. Its transport, gate and correction make the predicted motion explicit. Move-and-correct is a simple and
general output parameterisation for latent world models.
\paragraph{Limitations.} As a plug-in to DINO-WM on Wall, whose single history frame leaves the head only predicted
features to transport, \ours{} raises latent error (by \dinowmWallErrInc\%). Content never observed relies on the correction, and forecasts are
deterministic. Language-Table test and training segments may share a play session. The surgical data are scene-camera
views of dVRK training tasks, not in-vivo endoscopy.
