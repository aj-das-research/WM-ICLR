\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\ours{} forecasts by moving what it has already seen: a gated local transport of measured foundation features plus a
small learned correction. It is the most accurate predictor on held-out real and surgical robot video, it improves
published world models when plugged into them, and its transport field and gate make the predicted motion explicit.

\paragraph{Limitations.} \ours{} predicts features, not pixels, so pixel metrics rely on a separately trained decoder.
Transport can only copy observed content, so newly revealed regions depend on the correction. The surgical benchmark
uses scene-camera views of dVRK training tasks, not in-vivo endoscopy.
