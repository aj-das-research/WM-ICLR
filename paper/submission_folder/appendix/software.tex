\subsection{Software}\label{app:software}
The forecaster, the plug-in head and the evaluation helpers are packaged as a small Python library, \texttt{shiftwm}
(MIT licence, depending only on PyTorch and NumPy), separate from the research code.
%
It exposes \texttt{ShiftWM}, the forecaster of \cref{sec:arch}, \texttt{ShiftHead}, the backbone-agnostic plug-in head
of \cref{app:plugin} with its gate closed at initialisation, \texttt{forecast}, which also returns the transport weights,
gate and correction, \texttt{transport\_field}, the expected source offset of every patch, and \texttt{skill}.
%
Its tests confirm that the package reproduces the research implementation (maximum absolute difference $0$ on the
trained DROID checkpoint) and that \texttt{ShiftHead} starts at the backbone's prediction.
