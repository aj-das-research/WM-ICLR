\textbf{Prespecified best, median and worst cases.} The episode-gain ranks among 141 DROID development recordings each show their first eligible window. AR denotes matched autoregression. Full RGB frames are recorded observations ($f10$) and withheld targets ($f60$). Maps show three-seed native, training-standardized $h=10$ feature MSE on one unclipped logarithmic scale.
