\textbf{Keep a direct path to the observation.} Autoregression feeds predicted features into the next prediction. ShiftWM (ours) instead mixes a fixed observed grid and adds a bounded correction at each horizon. Both paths are conditioned on supplied actions; the detailed conditioning and decoder appear in Figure~\ref{fig:editorial-spatial-method}.
