\subsection{Where the gains come from}\label{app:analysis}
\Cref{tab:regions} (\cref{app:pixel}) gives the moving/static split behind the first paragraph of \cref{sec:analysis}.
Below we show what the forecasts look like and how the gain varies across episodes.
\paragraph{Sharpness of the forecasts (supports Proposition~\ref{prop:residual}).} An additive head must write out the
feature contrast along the motion. \Cref{fig:mechanism}a shows each method's $k{=}10$ forecast on the teaser window
(selection rule in \cref{app:qualitative}), coloured by one PCA shared by all methods, and (b) gives the spatial
contrast each forecast keeps relative to the truth: at $k{=}10$, Direct and AR retain less of it than \ours{}.
