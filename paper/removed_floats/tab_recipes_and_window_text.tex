\subsection{Additional ablations}\label{app:ablations}
\Cref{sec:ablations} varies the architecture of \ours{} on DROID; this subsection varies the training recipe and relates
the window and memory rows of \cref{tab:ablations}.
\input{submission_folder/tables/recipes.tex}
\paragraph{Training recipes.} Because halving training to 8k steps barely changes validation error (\cref{tab:recipes}), we
tested whether stronger regularisation or more data helps: training on
both exterior cameras with EMA weights, dropout and memory-token dropout, and adding explicit correlation (cost-volume)
features between consecutive observed grids to every method. Recipes were chosen on validation (seed 0), and the
test split is scored once. With early stopping, \ours{} and Direct stopped at 3k/4k steps and AR ran 8k; AR-TF and some
correlation-feature rows are incomplete (--).
\paragraph{Window and memory size.} The window rows of \cref{tab:ablations} vary the transport window
($w\in\{1,5,7\}$ and a global window over the whole grid; $w{=}1$ keeps the gate and correction but cannot move content)
and the memory row the number of source frames ($S\in\{1,3\}$).
