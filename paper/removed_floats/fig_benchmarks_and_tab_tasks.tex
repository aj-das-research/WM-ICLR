\begin{minipage}[c]{0.5\linewidth}
\Cref{sec:intro} summarises the evaluation along four axes; \cref{fig:benchmarks} shows them with their
metrics, and \cref{tab:tasks} states, for every benchmark, what the data show and what the world model is asked to do. In all
forecasting benchmarks the task is the same: given the last $H{=}3$ observed frames (encoded to frozen DINOv2 patch
grids), the actions between them and the next $K$ action blocks, predict the patch features of the next $K$ frames,
without seeing them. The benchmarks differ in embodiment, scene and action space. In the planning benchmarks the world
model is used inside a planner, and the task is to reach a goal image.
\end{minipage}
