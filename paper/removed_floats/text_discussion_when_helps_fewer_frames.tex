\paragraph{When transport helps.} The gains are largest where content moves within the transport window and keeps its
appearance, as on DROID, where the advantage holds in every decile of motion; on the surgical tasks the instruments move
less within ten steps, and \ours{} is best on every task.

\paragraph{Towards fewer observed frames.} As a plug-in to DINO-WM on Wall, where the backbone conditions on a single
history frame, the head raises latent error by \dinowmWallErrInc\%, and restricting \ours{} itself to one source frame
raises its DROID error by \ablsone\%.
%
Content that no observed frame contains can only come from the correction, as the worst window in \cref{fig:rollouts}
shows, and motion larger than the window, such as camera ego-motion, lies outside the transport prior.

Longer observed histories and larger or adaptive windows are the natural extensions.
