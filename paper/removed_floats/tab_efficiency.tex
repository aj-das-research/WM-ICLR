\subsection{Efficiency}\label{app:cost}
\Cref{sec:intro} states that transport adds $<\!0.3\%$ parameters to a matched predictor; this subsection gives the full
cost comparison.
\input{submission_folder/tables/efficiency.tex}
\Cref{tab:efficiency} compares parameters, compute per forecast step and
training time. Transport adds 0.05M parameters (0.2\%). Because \ours{} and Direct decode all $K$ steps in
parallel, a $K$-step forecast needs one decoder pass, whereas recursive predictors need $K$, and they also train about
$1.7{\times}$ longer.
