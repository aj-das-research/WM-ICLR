Recorded simulator observations compare the historical ShiftWM (ours) context model with Framewise, at training seed 0 on development $(v_1,p_1)$. These are the original first-lexicographic cases from four outcome strata, not success-frequency estimates.
Each card shares pixel-identical goal and support observations across methods. Success/failure labels give the actual native stopping call; endpoints are not synchronized. The scored goal is the gray block and blue pusher in the goal image. The persistent green T is an unscored reset marker.
Block and pusher errors use native $512\times512$ coordinates despite the $224\times224$ recorded images. Success requires their combined position-error norm below 20 pixels and wrapped block-angle error below $\pi/9$. In (b), 1.2 pixels of block error does not compensate for 429.0 pixels of pusher error.
In (d), every method reaches the goal during shared support at call 2; the support and terminal image is identical and shown once. No CEM plan executes.
