## Three real alternatives

**A — three matched endpoint cards plus distinct support-only card (selected).**
Four outcomes fit5.5×3.139in with8pt type. The three eligible cards keep exact
per-method diagnostics adjacent to observations; the fourth devotes its space
to the crucial no-planning/ineligible boundary and shows only two real images.
This retains all scored components and makes the429px pusher failure visible.

**B — four-row shared-column table.** The goal, support and outcomes align down
the page, but the numeric diagnostics in a separate right column require more
cross-referencing and method identity is less immediate. It provides no gain
in photo size at the same height. All three layouts now pass text geometry.

**C — three eligible case columns plus support band.** Larger individual images
make the scenes easy to see, but the available width/height omits component
error rows. Those values explain the visually misleading block-only success,
so A is preferred for this task.
