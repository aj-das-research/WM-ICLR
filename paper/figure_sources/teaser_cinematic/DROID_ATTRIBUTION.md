# DROID recorded observations

Recorded images and commands: DROID, https://droid-dataset.github.io/, licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Preserve this attribution with reused examples. Dataset authors retain their credits and rights. Source inventory and immutable object generations are recorded in reports/evidence/real_video/droid_selected_inventory.json; replay.json binds the exact processed observations and all source ledgers.

The 39 stored source frames and 12 exported PNGs are unchanged recorded observations. Some frames are model-visible support; the later frames are evaluator-only ground truth. Model outputs are numerical visual features, never generated RGB images. The derived feature errors, stored predictions and transport diagnostics come from this project's completed, verified three-seed spatial campaign. No human demonstrations are presented as autonomous actions chosen by our model.
