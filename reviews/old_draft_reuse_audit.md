# Old-draft reuse audit (ShiftWM, 2026-09-25)

**Scope.** This audit covers the old draft `paper/main.tex` with `paper/sections/*`, `paper/figures/*`, `paper/generated/*`, `paper/tables/*`, `paper/evidence/*` and `world_model_draft.pdf`, plus `results/real_video`, `results/v2r2` and the aggregated `reports/*` that the old evidence ledgers point to. There is no `results/v1*`. It compares them against the current submission (`paper/iclr2027.tex` + `paper/submission_folder/`, results in `results/v2s` and `results/v2`). This is an inventory only. No paper or result file was changed.

## What model produced the old results

- **Old "ShiftWM" (compact spatial variant).** DINOv2-S features pooled to a **4×4** grid (2×2 was supplementary). A per-patch temporal predictor. A dense row-convex mixing over the *last* frame only, with a +4I identity bias. A **tanh-bounded** correction. It was trained for 30 epochs, and every number is scored on the 4×4 native grid (the ten-step endpoint). This produced all of the main text of the old draft: the DROID table and ablations, the capacity control, the IWS single-image study, the RGB-decoder study, the PushT/Reacher "current-model planning" and the qualitative figures.
- **Historical two-context model.** A LeWM causal predictor with FiLM context, **2×2 pooled** (1,536-D) targets and K=5 (plus a 10-block extrapolation). It produced the real-video study, the residual calibration, the fresh-session confirmation, the simulator composition study, goal calibration and the drone/surgery extensions.
- **Neither model is ShiftWM-v2.** ShiftWM-v2 uses 16×16 patches, a memory encoder, an AdaLN query decoder with a GRU action prefix, a 7×7 local transport over 3 source frames, and a gate plus correction.
- **Consequence.** Under rule (a), every model-comparison number in the old draft fails. What can be reused is limited to three kinds of item:
  - model-agnostic data audits,
  - harness and reproduction checks,
  - mathematics or protocols.
- **No v2 content in the old draft's history.** `git log -S` on `paper/main.tex` finds no v2 content (no `7\times7` and no `16\times16`) in any commit from 2026-09-19 to 2026-09-22.

## Summary

- **Reuse as is:** item 1 (DROID audit statistics; the splits are verified identical), item 3 (goal-cost bound, as a remark), item 5 (the v2r2 recipe rows are already present, but add the caveat about early-stopped runs and pending cells), and the definitions only from item 7.
- **Reuse as a separately labelled experiment:** item 2 (the released-evaluator LeWM check; disclose the Reacher 39 vs 42 difference), item 4 (the fresh-session protocol, only after re-running v2s checkpoints) and item 6 (AdaJEPA; optional).
- **Do not reuse (tempting cases):**
  - all old DROID, IWS and planning numbers, which come from the 4×4/tanh variant or the 2×2 context model;
  - all old qualitative, method, teaser and goal-geometry visuals;
  - the adapted DINO-WM results;
  - the random-action control, which should instead be recomputed under the official protocol.
